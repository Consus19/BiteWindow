How to lower your risk

CDC + EPA GUIDANCE

Research-backed prevention steps. These reduce exposure but do not guarantee that you won't be bitten.

🧴 Choose an EPA-registered repellent

Check for an EPA Reg. No. and use as labeled. CDC-listed ingredients include DEET, picaridin, IR3535, OLE/PMD and 2-undecanone. Do not use OLE/PMD on children under age 3.

👖 Cover exposed skin

Wear loose-fitting long sleeves, pants and socks. Permethrin-treated clothing and gear can add protection; never apply permethrin directly to skin.

💧 Drain breeding sites

Once a week empty, scrub, cover, or discard containers holding water, including buckets, toys, plant saucers, and birdbaths.

🏠 Strengthen your barriers

Repair window and patio screens; use air conditioning or intact screened areas when possible.
